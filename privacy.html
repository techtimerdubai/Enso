Privacy Policy

Effective 10 July 2026 · Last updated 10 July 2026

The short version: Ensō collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you draw stays on your own device. There is no server to send it to.

Ensō ("the app", "we") is a free drawing app made for everyone, including children. Privacy isn't a feature we added — it's how the app is built. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your information. (Spoiler: it never leaves your device.)

Information we collect

None. Ensō has no sign‑up, no login, no account, and no user profiles. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal detail. We do not use cookies for tracking, and there are no analytics, advertising, or third‑party tracking tools of any kind inside the app.

Where your work is stored

Your drawings and settings are saved locally on your own device, using your browser or device's private storage, so your work is still there when you come back. This on‑device data includes:

  • Your current drawing (so it auto‑saves and reopens)
  • Your recently used colours
  • A small note of whether you've seen the intro or the "install" tip, so we don't show them repeatedly
  • A cached copy of the app's own files, so it works offline

This information stays on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, because the app has no way to send it — its built‑in security policy blocks all outside network connections.

Photos and imported images

If you add a photo to the canvas, it is read and drawn entirely on your device. Ensō does not upload, copy, or transmit your photos anywhere. When you close or clear the canvas, that image is gone from the app.

Saving and sharing

When you choose to export (as PNG, SVG, video, or an .enso.json file) or use your device's Share button, you are the one deciding where that file goes. Ensō simply hands the finished file to your device; we never see it. Sharing happens through your own device's tools, under their own privacy terms.

Children's privacy

Ensō is safe for children. Because we collect no personal information at all, the app complies with children's privacy rules such as COPPA (US) and GDPR‑K (EU) by design. There is no behavioural advertising, no data collection, and no way for a child to reveal personal information to us — there is nothing to reveal it to. There is no chat, no community feed, and no user‑to‑user contact.

Optional donations

Ensō is free forever. The optional "Support" screen shows cryptocurrency wallet addresses you may choose to copy into your own wallet app. These are voluntary, peer‑to‑peer tips: there is no payment processor and no backend, so Ensō does not see, record, or process any payment, and collects no financial information.

Links that leave the app

Ensō contains a link to its source‑code page on GitHub. If you tap it, you leave Ensō and open that website, which has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the practices of external sites.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are always in full control. Clearing the app's data (or your browser data for the site), or uninstalling the app, permanently removes everything. We keep no copy, so there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing we could delete on our end.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since the app collects no data, any change would only ever clarify wording — not expand what we gather.
